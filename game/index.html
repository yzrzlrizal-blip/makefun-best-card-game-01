EMBER COURT

A duel of cards beneath the Cinder Warden's gaze.

You and the Warden hold 16 life each. Play cards with mana — strikes to wound, siphons to drink life back, guards to raise shield, mends to heal, rallies to draw. Play two cards of one suit in a turn and the second resonates for +2 power. First ember out wins the court.
